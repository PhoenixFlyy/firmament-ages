# Changelog staging: Chronicle B2

Player-facing, English, for the next version's section in `CHANGELOG.md`. Branch `b2/content` on top of 0.13.0.

- **Every quest pays real items.** Random and choice reward tables are gone. Each quest that asks for work pays fixed items of its Age besides experience: materials for the shared base go to whoever claims them first, gear and food come to each of you. Keystones pay a package, goals pay gear and a keepsake (a copper sword and shield, a copper backpack, a cloud in a bottle, strider shoes, a crystal heart, a glider, a blue steel sword, an imperium apple, the dragon egg), the proofs of work a keepsake for each of you. Rewards never hand you a task item of an open quest, a sky material, a token, an offering or the Age's tally metal. 333 quests re-rewarded.
- **Side Roads,** a new optional group: one chapter per Age with rows of four to seven quests, each row a craft of one mod that branches off a quest of the Age (the first quest says which). 59 roads, 301 quests, from Strange Beasts in Dawn to Crystals and Rings in the Singularity Age; every mod that is not decoration now has its story in the Chronicle. Each road quest pays an item of its Age.
- **The Great Works branch off their Ages.** The Gatehouse hangs on "Flux Mill" (Bronze Age), The Living House on "Brass" (Iron Age), The Silo on "Launch Pad" (Space Age); each Age map carries a link to the plan. Their rewards no longer hand you task items of the Age (copper, casings, large cogwheels, analog levers, a bronze shield; brass sheets, brass funnels, a cuckoo clock; fluid ducts, gantry shafts, pulse extenders).
- **The road is paced.** 32 one-click quests folded into their neighbours (loose rocks into "Stone Tools", straw into "Thatch", the raw hide into "Hunter", clay knapping into "Potter", the small vessel into "Pottery for Metal", the pickaxe head into "Copper Pickaxe", the jug into "A Pot of Soup", the unfired idol into "Hearth Idol", the stone anvil into "Forging", the quern into "In Flux", the mineral prospector into "Prospector", the crucible into "Bronze", shafts and cogs into "Mechanical Saw", the fuel heater into "Brass", the barrel into "Genuine Leather", the dictionary into "White Chalk", sal ammoniac into "Calcination Oven", the incubator into "The Three Principles", the divination rod into "Mercury Catalyst", copper wire and connectors into "Grid Online (LV)", crude oil into "Pumpjack", lubricant into "Distillation Tower", circuit boards into "Electronics", the infuser into "Steel Casing", the data model into "Deep Learner", the fabricator into "Simulation", the hohlraum into "Fusion Fuel", hypercharged alloy into "Subatomic Alloy", atomic alloy into "Awakened Keystone", refined naquadah into "Stargate Blocks"); "Zinc Nuggets" and "Mercury" are gone (Mercury waits for the naquadah of the Singularity Age), "Nether Stars" of the Quantum Age too (the fabricator prints them in the Information Age).
- **New and bigger steps:** Copper Kit (axe, hammer, knife, shovel and saw of copper, the keystone of the First Metal strand; axe and hammer head mold patterns drawn), Bronze Kit (pickaxe, axe, saw, hammer and chisel), Flux Mill (32 flux ground and dropped into a vessel by a chute), Winter Gear (all four leather pieces), 32 charcoal, 16 tin and 8 zinc, 16 andesite alloy, 16 of each processor, four 64k components, Resource Crops as a step of the Factory strand, Ore Tripling (64 iron clumps from the running chamber), Factory Line (an advanced smelting and an advanced enriching factory), 64 prediction matrices, Wither on Demand (four Nether Stars printed from a Wither model), 64 HDPE, 24 polonium pellets in "Reactor Online". The Machine Route of the Arcane Age carries the blaze burner and the blaze enchanter as steps; "Blaze Cake" is its side path.
- **"Beyond the Firmament"** on the road of offerings appears with the Singularity Age.
- **Maps:** titles no longer sit on other titles or quests (the layout keeps room for them), the five-strand chapters leave the entry with clear lanes, every chapter stays within two line crossings. The Side Roads chapters are drawn as rows.
- **Fixed texts:** crushing wheels give about 1.5 times the metal, the crusher two and a half times.
- Walkthrough (`docs/WALKTHROUGH.md`) synced with the strands, counts and rewards.

### Technical

- `dev/gen_quests.py`: fixed rewards `T()`/`P()` on every `Q(...)`, `quest_rewards()` stops on a work quest without items; `ROADS` and `build_roads()` (group `roads`, chapters `roads_<age>`, tags `[<stage>, roads]`); `WORK_HOOKS`, `quest_link()` (FTB Quests `quest_links`, javap 2101.1.36) and cross-chapter dependencies of the Great Works plans; reward tables, `r_random`, `r_choice` and `supplies`/`comfort` removed; `reward_tables/` is no longer written.
- `dev/quest_layout.py`: label room in `relax()` (`LABEL_*`), `labels` and `satellites` in the chapter spec. `dev/validate_quests.py`: Side Roads chapters and quest links are checked, roads are no Age chapters. `dev/preview_quests.py`: draws quest links, a letter glyph where the flat renderer has no texture (entity models, custom loaders, mods missing from the client folder), a red dot for a quest without any icon. `dev/qart_textures.py`: block models under any parent path, model-chain textures, textures named like the item.
- `dev/gen_quest_images.py`: knapping pictures `axe_head_mold`, `hammer_head_mold`, `knife_blade_mold`, `shovel_head_mold`.
- `dev/age_map.toml`: `ars_nouveau:wixie_charm` moved to `[derived_hidden]` (it came only from a reward table); stage locks regenerated.
