# Firmament Ages 0.5.0

First public release on CurseForge. Beta: the whole progression is playable from Dawn to the final boss, balance and texts will still change.

## Content

- **Dawn and ten Ages**, Stone to Singularity Age, each with its quest chapter on **K** (main strands, side lines, one goal quest) and its signature item.
- **The Shrine of Caelum** with rings 0 to 8: offerings, rites, team prayer, the Age ceremony and one blessing per ring. Each accepted ring is consecrated into one sky-marble structure with the Age's colour accent; it cannot be broken, except in maintenance mode (sneak and punch the Shrine Heart with an empty hand), which gives back the original blocks. Blessings: sanctuary against monster spawns, then faster walking, more health, more XP, faster mining, longer reach, softer falls and luck for players near the shrine. The Arcane Keystone can be borrowed in the Quantum Age.
- **Age gating everywhere:** locked items, blocks and recipes are hidden in EMI; machines of every mod refuse recipes of locked Ages; miners and prospecting tools only find the ores of your Ages; mobs and dimensions open Age by Age.
- **Finale:** Ultimate Singularity by Draconic fusion, a Classic Stargate, The Origin, gateway waves and The Primordial.
- **Firmament Ages Core 0.6.0**, the pack's own mod (shrine, gate, reactor controller, The Origin).
- Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.252, 193 mods.

## Known issues

- Draconic Evolution and Stargate Journey are beta mods on 1.21.1. Back up your world before the Space Age.
- Unlocking an Age reloads the server's datapacks: expect a freeze of a few seconds during the ceremony.
- Gateways to Eternity runs the CurseForge build of 5.1.0, which is older than the Modrinth build of the same version; report anything odd in the final fight.
