# Firmament Ages: Walkthrough

From the first rock to the last gate. One fellowship, one shrine, nine offerings, one fight.

## Overview

| Age | Goal (offering) | Key stations | How the next Age opens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dawn | The First Spark: a firestarter and a lit firepit | knapping grid, 2x2 crafting grid | The goal quest opens the Stone Age. No shrine yet. |
| Stone Age | Hearthstone | pit kiln, firepit, small vessel, ceramic molds, charcoal pit | Ring 0, Hearth Circle. Rite: kindle the heart. Opens the Bronze Age. |
| Bronze Age | Sky Disc | forge, stone, copper and bronze anvils, quern, crucible, water wheel, millstone, mechanical saw | Ring 1, Bronze Sanctum. Rite: ring the bell. Opens the Iron Age. |

| Finale | Defeat The Primordial, the final boss in The Origin | the altar and arena of The Origin | No shrine. The boss's death grants `finale_won` to all of you. |

### The shrine in brief (Stone Age to Quantum Age)

Sneak is Left Ctrl here; Left Shift sprints.

1. One shrine per server. Leave 21 by 21 blocks free around the heart: every Age adds one ring outside the last.
   Ring N is (2N+5) blocks square; its plinth sits in the middle of one side, N+2 blocks from the heart.
2. Every ring needs a new Offering Plinth: four cobblestone, one on top of three.
3. Right-click the heart with an empty hand. It says what Caelum wants next and shows every missing block as a ghost.
   When the ring is whole, the heart glows.
4. Lay the offering on the new plinth (or right-click the heart with it). A wrong item is refused and the right one named.
   Sneak and right-click the plinth with an empty hand to take it back, until the prayer ends.
5. Do the Age's rite.
6. Pray: sneak and hold right-click on the heart, main hand empty, within 6 blocks. 10 s alone, 5 s with two, never less
   than 4 s. Progress drains while nobody prays.
7. The ceremony takes 12 s: the music stops, a beam in the Age's colour, the sky tints, the title "The ... Age
   dawns", Caelum's line, the blessing. "The sky shifts. Hold still." while the world changes. Then the new ring turns into
   consecrated stone, from the heart outward, in about 6 s. The new chapter appears in the Chronicle (K).
8. Each awakened ring adds one blessing. Blessings and the monster ward work within 12 blocks of the heart, plus 4
   per further Age (44 at the end). A broken ring pauses them. An Age is never taken away.
9. A thin beam in the colour of the latest Age stands over a lit, whole shrine. A dark beam means a broken ring.
   The beam is the way home from far off. A friend who was away sees the short ceremony of the new Age at his
   next login.
10. Wayshrines: ruined shrines of Caelum lie in the wide world, the first a few hundred blocks from spawn. Right-click
    the heart to learn the way to the nearest. Right-click its core to see the missing blocks, rebuild it from the
    stone around it, lay the offering of your Age (Bronze Age: a bronze double ingot; Iron Age: a meteoric iron
    ingot; Arcane Age: a meteoric iron double ingot; Industrial Age: a meteoric iron sheet; from the Electric Age:
    a starsteel ingot), do the rite and pray. A lit Wayshrine carries your blessings within 24 blocks and stays lit
    for good. Meteoric iron and starsteel come from fallen stars (Starfall).

## Dawn

### What you must reach
The First Spark: craft a firestarter and light a firepit. The quest itself opens the Stone Age for everyone.

### The way
1. Loose rocks lie everywhere. Right-click one with an empty hand. Collect about ten.
2. Twigs lie under trees: pick them up with an empty hand; each crafts into a stick. Leaves cut with a stone knife
   drop sticks too. Eight sticks.
3. Knap: hold two or more loose rocks of one kind and right-click into the air. A 5x5 grid opens. A click knocks a
   square away for good; the first click uses one rock; closing the grid early wastes it. The axe head
   (X stays, - is knocked away):
   ```
   - X - - -
   X X X X -
   X X X X X
   X X X X -
   - X - - -
   ```
   Put the head above a stick, twig or bone in the 2x2 grid: a stone axe. Knife (two blades per rock), hammer,
   shovel, hoe and javelin heads are in the quest "Knapping Patterns". There is no stone pickaxe.
4. Stone: fell a tree with the stone axe. Keystone "Stone Kit".
5. Shelter: cut tall grass with the stone knife for straw. Four straw make one thatch; make eight. Right-click two
   thatch blocks side by side with a large raw hide: a thatch bed. Keystone "Shelter".
6. Hunt: knap a javelin head, set it above a stick, kill any wild animal. Large prey drops large hides; carry a knife
   to butcher. Keystone "Raw Hide".
7. Goal: craft a firestarter from two sticks. Throw a log, three sticks and some straw on one block and hold
   right-click on it with the firestarter until the firepit lights. The Stone Age dawns for all of you: a short
   ceremony, and Caelum says "A spark in the dark. I have seen you."

### Pitfalls
- **Hands are not tools.** In Dawn logs, raw and hardened rock, ores and sandstone do not break without the right
  tool: no crack, nothing happens, one hint per minute. The stone axe and the stone hammer fell trees. With no stone
  pickaxe, rock and ore stay whole until the Stone Age. From then on the hand works again, slowly and without drops.
- Dawn withholds clay, pit kilns and metal. The crafting grid only makes stone and obsidian tools, the firestarter,
  straw, thatch, sticks and rope.
- **Darkness.** Caves are pitch black. Nights follow the moon; a new moon is nearly black. A torch lights your way in
  hand; a placed torch burns about 36 minutes.
- Something hunts in deep, dark caves until the Iron Age.
- The thatch bed sets your spawn but never skips the night. Beds that do come with the Iron Age.
- No recipe viewer, no block tooltip and no coordinates on F3 until the Bronze Age. The Chronicle (K) is the only
  list. Top left, a small sign names your Age and its goal; hold H to see it again.
- No thirst. A plain diet does no harm; a varied one makes you hardier. Food rots and tools wear.
- At night zombies and spiders walk the surface.

## Stone Age

### What you must reach
Offer the Hearthstone at the shrine. Raise the shrine first: the Shrine Heart in the Hearth Circle (ring 0).

### The way
1. Clay lies under grass near water. Athyrium fern, canna, goldenrod, pampas grass, perovskia and water canna
   grow above it. Knap five clay into a vessel, jug, bowl or mold.
2. Pit kiln: dig a hole one block deep. Hold an unfired piece, look at the floor of the hole and press V (the
   place key); up to four pieces fit. Right-click the pile with eight straw (or two thatch), then with eight logs,
   and light the top with a firestarter. After about six minutes the pottery is fired. The kiln reaches 1400 °C.
3. Fire an ingot mold and a pickaxe head mold. Keystone "Mold Set".
4. Copper ore pieces (native copper, malachite, tetrahedrite) lie on the surface above copper veins.
5. Charcoal pit: sneak and right-click the ground with logs for a log pile and right-click it with more logs.
   Cover every side with dirt, leave the top open, light it, then close the gap with dirt. When the smoke stops,
   dig the charcoal out with a shovel. The Shrine Heart and the Hearthstone need charcoal.
6. Fill the small vessel with copper ore and fire it in a pit kiln: the copper melts at 1080 °C. A firepit never
   gets hot enough: its fuels burn at 1000 °C at most, logs at about 600 to 760 °C. Take the vessel out while it
   glows and right-click it; put a fired mold in the slot and the copper pours in. Each small piece holds
   10 units of metal, an ingot 100: ten small pieces make one ingot. When the mold has cooled, right-click it to
   take the ingot out. Two ingots, then the pickaxe head mold the same way; set the head on a stick: a copper
   pickaxe. Keystone "Copper Pickaxe".
7. Camp: break wild crops for seeds, till and plant; fire a jug and fill it at a river; cook a soup in a ceramic pot
   on the firepit; fill a large vessel with food and seal it. Keystone "Stocked Camp".
8. Knap clay into the Unfired Hearth Idol and fire it in a pit kiln. Fire **two**: one for the heart, one for the
   Hearthstone.
   ```
   - X X X -
   X X X X X
   - X X X -
   - X X X -
   X X - X X
   ```
9. Build a workbench: the shrine recipes need its 3x3 grid (the inventory grid is 2x2).
   - Knap a saw blade mold from five clay, fire it, cast copper into it from the glowing vessel. The blade above
     a stick is a saw.
     ```
     - - X X X
     - - - X X
     X - - - X
     X - - - -
     X X X - -
     ```
   - A saw and a log make eight lumber; four lumber make planks; four planks make a workbench.
   - Four loose rocks in the 2x2 grid make a cobblestone.
10. Craft in the workbench (H Hearth Idol, S cobblestone, C charcoal, K copper ingot, - empty):
    ```
    Shrine Heart    Offering Plinth    Hearthstone
    - C -           - S -              - K -
    S H S           S S S              C H C
    - S -                              - K -
    ```
11. Place the heart where 21 by 21 blocks are free. Build the Hearth Circle (5x5, 3 high): eight cobblestone around
    the heart; on each corner a log post two high with thatch (or hay) on top; the plinth in the middle of one side,
    two blocks from the heart. The heart glows when the circle is whole. Keystone "Raise the Shrine".
12. Lay the Hearthstone on the plinth. Kindle the heart with a firestarter (flint and steel, a fire charge or a
    torch work too). Pray.
13. The Bronze Age dawns. Caelum keeps the Hearthstone. Blessing **Hearthward**: no monster spawns naturally near the
    heart. The circle turns to consecrated stone, and from now on a thin beam over the heart shows the way home.

After the ceremony the recipe viewer, the block tooltip and F3 coordinates appear, and tin, zinc, bismuth, silver,
gold, graphite, borax and the salts wake.

### Pitfalls
- The recipe viewer is still closed. The steps above and the Chronicle's notes ("Clay Patterns", "A Home") show the
  recipes on the way to the Hearthstone and every home block of this Age.
- The calendar tab opens in the inventory.
- Mining raw stone can collapse the ceiling. Support beams come with the Bronze Age.
- **Consecration.** After its prayer a ring is consecrated: unbreakable by players, machines, explosions, pistons,
  fluids and bosses. To change it, sneak and punch the heart with an empty hand: 60 s of maintenance, in which
  consecrated blocks break and give back their original block. Punch again to close it. Caelum does not listen during
  maintenance.
- A hole breaks the ring: blessings pause, prayers are refused, the Ages stay. Put the original material (or a
  consecrated block of the same kind) back.
- One heart per server. Breaking it needs sneaking and a pickaxe; a heart placed again finds its plinths.
- Food rots. Seal large vessels, salt, dry and cook.

## Bronze Age

### What you must reach
Offer the Sky Disc at the shrine, in the Bronze Sanctum (ring 1).

### The way
1. Smithing: build a charcoal forge: a hole one block deep lined with stone on four sides and below, charcoal
   right-clicked into it seven or eight layers high, open sky above, lit with a firestarter. Fuel goes in the
   forge's lower slots, the metal to heat in the upper ones. Make a stone anvil with a stone hammer on raw igneous
   rock. On an anvil, pick the plan from the scroll, strike until the green marker meets the red one, and end
   with the strikes the rules on top ask for (note "Anvil Work"). Craft a quern and a handstone; grind borax, lime
   or other flux stones into flux (4). Weld two hot ingots into a double ingot. Weld copper double ingots into a
   copper anvil. Keystone "Copper Anvil".
2. Prospecting: cassiterite pieces point to tin, sphalerite pieces to zinc. Smith a prospector's pick; build a
   mineral prospector (it shows direction and size of a vein). Saw support beams and set them in your mine. Cast
   4 tin and 2 zinc ingots. Keystone "First Mine".
3. Alloys: mix clay with kaolinite and graphite powder into fire clay; knap and fire a crucible. Melt copper with
   tin (or bismuth, or gold and silver) into bronze. Smith a bronze tool, then a bronze anvil. Keystone
   "Bronze Anvil". Only a bronze anvil forges the bronze double sheet.
4. Kinetics:
   - One zinc ingot crafts into nine zinc nuggets. There is no way back.
   - Andesite alloy from andesite cobble and zinc nuggets. Make four. Andesite is a grey volcanic rock (igneous
     extrusive, with basalt, rhyolite and dacite). If your land has none, travel: rock regions change every few
     hundred blocks.
   - A water wheel in flowing water, 8 shafts and 4 cogwheels, a mechanical saw.
   - A millstone on a water wheel. Keystone "First Mill". It grinds what the quern grinds.
   - The press turns a double ingot into a sheet. A fan blowing through water washes ore deposits: the pan's
     work, by machine.
5. Craft the Sky Disc: a bronze double sheet in the middle, a gold sheet on each side.
6. Craft a new plinth. Raise the Bronze Sanctum (7x7) around the circle, at the heart's level: rock bricks (19), a
   bronze block on each corner, the plinth in the middle of one side three blocks from the heart, and a bronze or
   brass bell on the brick opposite the plinth.
7. Lay the Sky Disc on the plinth, ring the bell, pray.
8. The Iron Age dawns. Blessing **Skyreading**: 5 % faster near the shrine.

### Pitfalls
- The recipe viewer and the block tooltip open now, without relogging: hover over an item, R shows its recipes,
  U its uses. F3 shows coordinates.
- **Hidden ores.** Ores of a later Age look like the rock they sit in and drop loose rock. The prospector's pick and
  the mineral prospector do not report them. Iron wakes with the Iron Age.
- Only andesite machines exist: water wheels, saws, presses, fans and mills. Their parts take bronze where they took
  iron. Crushing wheels, deployers, brass, trains and steam come with the Iron Age.
- Ring the bell after the Bronze Age has dawned. A ring from before the Hearthstone's ceremony does not count.
- Skeletons and creepers join the nights.

## Iron Age

### What you must reach
Offer the Steel Heart at the shrine, in the Iron Sanctum (ring 2).

### The way
1. Metallurgy: build a bloomery from bronze double sheets and stone bricks. Fill it with iron ore and charcoal: a
   raw iron bloom. Hammer it into wrought iron (4). Smith a wrought iron anvil. Build a blast furnace: iron ore,
   charcoal and flux give pig iron. Hammer pig iron into high carbon steel, then steel. Keystone: 16 steel ingots.
2. Power & Motion: brass (copper and zinc, in the crucible or a heated basin); a precision mechanism by sequenced
   assembly; crushing wheels (ore pieces to dust, about 1.7 times the metal); a fuel heater for heated mixing; a
   steam engine on a boiler. Keystone: 32 wrought iron ingots from a machine-fed bloomery line.
3. Survival: a barrel; leather (limewater, scrape, tannin); one leather armour piece; four sealed large vessels in a
   cellar below ground. Keystone "Winter Stores": tick it when the cellar holds enough for all of you.
4. Frontier: the Frontier Compass (four wrought iron ingots around a magnetite ore piece). Right-click with it: it
   names the direction and the distance of the nearest Cold Forge, a ruined smithy of granite in the hills and
   highlands. Its chest keeps a Frontier Shard (a team that finds a forge earlier keeps the shard too). Three passes
   on the deployer and the press (a steel sheet, then a wrought iron double sheet) finish it into the Frontier Relic.
   Keystone: the Frontier Relic.
5. Craft the Steel Heart: a steel sheet on top, a wrought iron double sheet left and right, the precision mechanism
   in the middle, the Frontier Relic below.
6. Raise the Iron Sanctum (9x9, 4 high): a smooth rock border at the heart's level; on each corner a pillar three
   smooth rock high with a wrought iron (or better) lamp on top; two wrought iron (or better) bars beside each
   pillar, one block above the border. The plinth: middle of one side, four blocks from the heart.
7. Lay the Heart on the plinth, light the four lamps, pray. A lamp without fuel goes out later.
8. The Arcane Age dawns. Blessing **Iron Will**: 5 % more maximum health near the shrine.

### Pitfalls
- **Beds** skip the night from now on; half of you in bed is enough.
- The map and the minimap open. The climate tab opens.
- The cave hunter is gone for good. Blood moons may now rise.
- The first winter comes about now: crops stop growing, it gets cold.
- No Age needs a boss. The Wither, the Ender Dragon, the Wilden Chimera and the Chaos Guardian stay optional
  shortcuts; every Age has its own way to what they drop.
- No food goes into any recipe or turn-in.
- The storage terminal can be built until the Information Age.
- Stars fall now on some clear nights: a streak, a boom, and a hint on screen ("A star falls to the north-east").
  The crater lies 300 to 2,500 blocks away. Its meteorites are the only meteoric iron; while the core glows (two
  days) they drop double. Missing a fall costs only the bonus.

## Arcane Age

### What you must reach
Bind the Arcane Keystone and offer it in the Spirit Circle (ring 3).

### The way
1. Spirits:
   - Datura seeds now drop from grass. They grow only on Arcane Soil: eight dirt around an amethyst make eight
     farmland; a hoe tills it again when it dries.
   - Datura fruit becomes Demon's Dream essence. Then the Dictionary of Spirits and white chalk.
   - No portal leads to the Nether here. Set at least seven offerings within five blocks of one spot (anvils, mob
     heads, gold or black steel blocks, piles of gold, pig iron or black steel ingots) and kill a goat, pig or sheep
     there with a scythe. A portal opens where you stand. Bring back 4 iesnium ingots.
   - Bind a Djinni into a book of binding. Keystone "Spirit Proof": a spirit attuned crystal and two iesnium ingots.
   - Next: Dimensional Storage and the Dimensional Mineshaft with a Djinni miner. It gives rich ore pieces of every
     metal your Age allows.
2. Source:
   - Novice spellbook, a sourcelink, an imbuement chamber, 16 source gems (imbue amethyst or lapis lazuli).
   - Enchanting apparatus, a Starbuncle (item transport), the apprentice spellbook.
   - Archwood saplings: any sapling ringed by four powders of one gem (lapis lazuli, ruby, emerald or amethyst). A
     sourceberry bush: a blueberry and two lapis lazuli powder.
   - A Wilden Tribute in the enchanting apparatus: a source gem block in the middle; two horns, two wings, two
     spikes of the Wilden, a spirit attuned crystal and a mercury shard on the pedestals (5,000 source). The Wilden
     Chimera drops one too. Keystone: the Wilden Tribute.
3. Alchemy: pyromantic brazier, calcination oven, sal ammoniac accumulator, liquefaction cauldron, digestion vat,
   distiller, incubator, divination rod. Keystone: the mercury catalyst. Ore pieces of every grade digest in the
   digestion vat with sal ammoniac (ten rich pieces give seven sulfur, two normal pieces one); each sulfur incubates
   into a whole dust, twice the metal of the pieces.
4. The Arcane Keystone, two ways:
   - On the Create line: a Hollow Keystone from a chest of a Nether fortress, three passes of a spout (250 mB of
     liquid experience), a deployer (a blaze rod) and a press.
   - In the Djinni crafting pentacle (bound Djinni book): spirit attuned crystal, iesnium ingot, Wilden Tribute,
     mercury catalyst, steel ingot, source gem block.
5. Raise the Spirit Circle (11x11, 4 high): a sourcestone border (35); on each corner an otherstone pillar three high
   capped with a source gem block; eight candles standing on the border. Plinth: five blocks from the heart.
6. Lay the Keystone, light the eight candles, pray.
7. The Industrial Age dawns. Blessing **Attunement**: 10 % more experience near the shrine.

### Pitfalls
- Datura breaks on ordinary farmland.
- One carrier per function: Starbuncles move items, Dimensional Storage stores them, alchemy multiplies ore, the
  Djinni crafts. The spirit crusher grinds no ore.
- Gem veins wake: lapis, amethyst, opal, pyrite, ruby, sapphire, topaz, emerald. Diamonds wait for the next Age.
- The mana bar appears now.
- The Quantum Age needs this Keystone (or a second one) again.

## Industrial Age

### What you must reach
Offer the Pressure Core in the Foundry Nave (ring 4).

### The way
1. Coke & Steel:
   - 16 treated wood (planks soaked in creosote).
   - Coke oven: 3x3x3 coke bricks, formed with the Engineer's Hammer. It cokes bituminous coal and lignite. 32 coal
     coke.
   - Blast bricks: three from nether bricks, fire bricks and a magma block. Build and form the blast furnace.
   - Arcane Gearbox: on the mechanical crafter a gearbox, four steel plates, two precision mechanisms and two steel
     components. With magic: four source gems, four steel plates and a gearbox in the grid; a Wixie can make it.
   - Reinforced blast bricks: four from four blast bricks, four steel plates and an Arcane Gearbox. Improved blast
     furnace with preheaters.
   - Black steel (8): alloy weak steel (steel, nickel, black bronze), weld it with pig iron, work it on the anvil.
     A black steel anvil.
   - A built-up steel cannon chamber: melt steel in a heated basin, cast the four steel layers in their moulds, bore
     them on the cannon drill, stack them with the cannon builder and heat them in a fan's blast over lava. It goes
     into the Core.
   - Keystone: two black steel double sheets.
2. Power: the alternator (rotation into FE, the only bridge); 8 copper wire; 4 LV connectors; 4 lead (galena wakes
   now); an LV capacitor. Keystone "Grid Online (LV)": alternator and capacitor placed and wired. The alternator
   needs a fuelled source: a steam engine on a boiler over a blaze burner with fuel (from the Electric Age also a
   diesel engine). Water wheels and windmills turn it but give no FE; with both on one shaft it gives the fuelled
   share.
3. Ore Line: a crusher (ore pieces to powder plus dust, twice the metal); a metal press (a sheet from two ingots);
   alloy bricks (two from sandstone and fire bricks) and the alloy kiln; 8 constantan. Keystone: 64 iron dust from a
   machine-fed crusher.
4. Logistics & Backfill: packager, frogport, stock ticker, factory gauge; the thresher (steel sheet metal, light
   engineering blocks, a redstone engineering block, a hopper, formed with the hammer). Keystone: 64 steel sheets
   delivered through the package network.
5. Craft the Pressure Core: a heavy engineering block on top, a black steel double sheet left and right, the Arcane
   Gearbox in the middle, the built-up steel chamber below.
6. Raise the Foundry Nave (13x13, 5 high): a coke brick border (43); on each corner a heavy engineering block, steel
   scaffolding three high and an electric lantern on top. Plinth: six blocks from the heart.
7. Lay the Core, power the four lanterns, pray.
8. The Electric Age dawns. Blessing **Tireless Hands**: you break blocks 5 % faster near the shrine.

### Pitfalls
- Your fellowship can now claim 25 chunks and keep them loaded. Lines in unloaded chunks stop.
- Multiblocks: build the shape from the Engineer's Manual, then hit the marked block with the Engineer's Hammer.
- Nickel (garnierite), diamond (kimberlite) and lead (galena) wake.
- The alloy kiln can be built only in this Age; LV and MV capacitors and the mechanical spawner until the
  Information Age. Built ones keep working.

## Electric Age

### What you must reach
Fuse the Humming Core in the arc furnace and offer it in the Tesla Crown (ring 5).

### The way
1. Power Grid: 8 electrum wire (MV), 8 steel wire (HV), a transformer and an HV transformer, the diesel generator
   (the one FE generator of this Age), 4 Alternating Flux wire. Keystone: two HV capacitors.
2. Oil: a core sample (it shows what lies under a chunk), a pumpjack over a reservoir, crude oil, a distillation
   tower, lubricant. Keystone: 16 buckets of diesel.
3. Aluminium & Excavator:
   - Bauxite wakes. Aluminium (16) comes only from the arc furnace.
   - Arc furnace steels: two steel, a black bronze and a nickel make four weak steel; weak steel and pig iron make
     black steel; five black steel, two steel, a brass and a rose gold make nine weak red steel. Weld and work red
     steel on the anvil, two sheets.
   - The excavator digs a chunk's mineral mix forever.
   - Keystone: 32 aluminium plates.
4. Circuits: 8 vacuum tubes, 4 circuit boards, the assembler (grid autocrafting). A Nether Star from the arc
   furnace (a wither skeleton skull with eight soul sand, a gold block and two electrum plates), from the Afrit
   crafting pentacle, or from the Wither. Keystone: 16 Attuned Circuits (a circuit board, a quartz, two advanced
   electronic components and electrum wire; with magic a circuit board and a spirit attuned crystal; let the
   assembler make them).
5. Fuse the Humming Core in the arc furnace: an HV capacitor, with an Attuned Circuit, four aluminium plates, two red
   steel sheets and a Nether Star.
6. Raise the Tesla Crown (15x15, 5 high): aluminium blocks on the border (51); on each corner a red steel block, an HV
   capacitor, aluminium scaffolding two high and a floodlight on top. Plinth: seven blocks from the heart.
7. Lay the Core. The four floodlights need power and a redstone signal. Charge the four capacitors with 1,000,000 FE
   together; nothing is drained. Pray.
8. The Information Age dawns. Blessing **Long Arm**: half a block more reach near the shrine.

### Pitfalls
- LV, MV and HV wire each take their own connectors; transformers join them. Overloaded wire burns.
- The arc furnace takes over alloying from the alloy kiln.
- The osmium vein wakes now. Prospect it: the Information Age needs osmium at once.
- Excavated ores of a locked Age come out as spoil.
- Afrit (side path): its book needs an HV coil block.

## Information Age

### What you must reach
Offer the Data Matrix in the Data Nave (ring 6).

### The way
1. Factory:
   - Osmium: melt the ore pieces in a vessel or crucible and cast them in an ingot mold. Eight ingots.
   - Steel casing: weld two osmium ingots into a double ingot, weld that with a wrought iron sheet.
   - Metallurgic infuser (alloys, control circuits; it also finishes weak red steel into red steel), purification
     chamber (three times the metal per ore piece), electrolytic separator, 8 HDPE (pressurized reaction chamber,
     from ethene), gas-burning generator (the FE generator of this Age).
   - Blue steel: weak blue steel from the arc furnace, welded and worked like red steel. Four sheets for the Matrix,
     more for the presses below.
   - Keystone: an advanced smelting factory.
2. Digital:

## Singularity Age

### What you must reach
Forge the Ultimate Singularity from the nine relics, build a Stargate around it and dial The Origin. There is no
offering.

### The way
1. **The Gathering.** All nine relics on their plinths, every ring whole, none lent, no maintenance. Pray at the
   heart, two of you together if two are online. Caelum: "Nine gifts. One light. Open the gate." The nine relics fall
   out above the heart; they never despawn and do not burn. The plinths keep glowing. The sign top left now reads
   "Goal: Beyond the Firmament".
2. Chaos comes from your own reactor: it hands its spent fuel out as chaos fragments (a full cycle about six large
   fragments). The reactor parts are fused at draconic level without chaos: the core around an antimatter pellet
   with two subatomic alloys, each stabilizer with an awakened core and an antimatter pellet. About four cycles make
   the chaos the Age needs: twelve chaotic injectors (one large and three medium fragments and an antimatter pellet
   each), the chaotic core, two fragments for the Singularity, one for the Coordinates. The Chaos Guardian in the
   End drops chaos shards too; it is optional.
3. The draconic reactor: 4 stabilizers, an injector, the reactor core, 4 awakened draconium blocks as fuel. The
   reactor controller (a stabilizer frame, two awakened draconium ingots, two elite control circuits) sits against a
   stabilizer or injector: it shuts the reactor down at 80 % conversion, swaps the fuel while it is cold and signals
   ready by redstone. Starting it is your job. Keystone "Reactor Online".
4. A chaotic core and a singular alloy.
5. The Ultimate Singularity, chaotic fusion: the chaotic core on the fusion core; Hearthstone, Sky Disc, Steel Heart,
   Awakened Keystone, Pressure Core, Humming Core, Data Matrix, Star Chart, Quantum Core, two large chaos fragments and the
   singular alloy in twelve injectors; 2,000,000,000 FE.
6. Naquadah lies on Mercury; mine 16 with the digital miner. In the arc furnace raw naquadah becomes a naquadah
   ingot, and a naquadah ingot with a steel ingot becomes two naquadah-iron alloy. The metallurgic infuser makes
   refined naquadah (with refined obsidian); the nucleosynthesizer makes pure naquadah from it (with antimatter).
7. Stargate: 14 ring blocks, 9 chevron blocks, one base block and a DHD. The base block: two naquadah-iron alloy, a
   communication crystal, two refined naquadah, the Ultimate Singularity, two transfer crystals, a control crystal.
   Assemble the gate; power it from the reactor through a flux gate. Keystone "Gate Online".
8. Coordinates of The Origin: paper, an ink sac and a chaos shard. Dial **9, 16, 21, 33, 2, 37**, then the point of
   origin.
9. Step through.

### Pitfalls
- Two Gatherings: the prayer at the shrine gives the relics back; the altar in The Origin starts the fight.
- The shrine names a missing relic by its Age, and refuses while one is lent: "Bring back every relic first." A
  relic missing because its Age came by command is accepted at once on its plinth. The Gathering happens once. A
  relic laid back can be taken again.
- The reactor can explode. Watch temperature and field strength, keep the shields fed, refuel only while it is cold.
  Refuelling is by hand.
- The Origin stays shut before this Age, whatever the way in: gate, portal, teleporter or command.
- Keep chaos shards: two go into the Ultimate Singularity, one into the Coordinates of The Origin.

## Finale

### What you must reach
Defeat The Primordial, the final boss in The Origin. Its fall grants `finale_won` to all of you.

### The way
1. You step out of the gate south of a floating arena and walk north past the DHD. Nine islands float around it, one
   per Age, each with its relic turning above it.
2. Everyone online stands within 6 blocks of the altar in the middle. The altar answers.
3. The Primordial wakes above the altar: dark glass, a keel below, a halo of nine lights. Its veil takes most of your
   damage; it opens only when you answer an attack.
   - The Dark Before: strike the keel while it rests on the floor after a slam, or shoot the node that glows white.
   - Nine Lights (from two thirds): the lanterns go out. Shoot the node whose beam sweeps the floor, or strike the
     core from below while it pulls you in.
   - Firmament Shatter (from one third): stars fall. Pick up the shards it sheds: right-click one to let the stars
     pass you, hold one in the off hand and strike, or throw one at it (sneak and right-click) to open the veil.
4. Expect six to ten minutes. After ten minutes the veil opens for less time, after twelve the stars fall everywhere.
5. It falls: "Beyond the Firmament". The MekaSuit and the Meka-Tool can now be made. The shrine's beam turns gold.
6. The gate at the arena dials home. Keep at least one Consecrated Shard ("A Piece of the Sky"): a shard lies on the
   floor for a minute, and once The Primordial has fallen no shard ever drops again. With four glass a shard makes
   four Firmament Glass.

### Pitfalls
- Every online player who is not a spectator must stand at the altar. One friend elsewhere and nothing starts.
  A player who has not moved or acted for five minutes is not counted.
- After a wipe it withdraws once nobody is near it for 30 s, and the lanterns return. Gather again after 30 s.
- The altar cannot be broken, not even by the boss.
- Nothing else spawns in the arena while The Primordial lives.
- Monsters spawn in The Origin only in darkness; the arena is lit. Beds and respawn anchors do not work there.
- The victory counts once. A friend who logs in later receives it silently.
