# Firmament Ages changelog

Beta: the whole progression is playable from Dawn to The Origin and its final boss, balance and texts will still change. Newest version on top; a CurseForge upload takes the newest section. The log reaches back to 0.5.0, the first release candidate, which was never published.

## 0.13.0

Links, endgame and the new Chronicle. Every Age's machines now take a part of the Age before, the Singularity Age gets its ore step, and the Chronicle opens with The Firmament: the shrine's build guides, pictures in the quests and three Great Works. **An existing world keeps working;** the new osmium vein and the new Wayshrine spacing only appear in new chunks.

- **The Firmament is the first chapter.** The Welcome chapter is gone; its read-me-first notes (waking, reading the Chronicle, keys, the sign in the corner, the fellowship, crafting by hand, what Dawn withholds, the shrine) open The Firmament. The offerings of every Age wind on one road to "Beyond the Firmament". Beside each offering a build guide appears when its Age dawns: the ring rising as an animation, then one page per layer with its blocks, the offering, the tithe and the rite. The shrine picture in each Age chapter opens the same guide.
- **Chapters that wind.** In every Age chapter the goal sits in the middle; the strands leave the entry and curve round it, side paths branch outward and the notes wait behind the entry. Your progress is kept: no quest changed its identity.
- **Pictures in the quests.** Knapping patterns are drawn as pictures (light squares stay, dark squares are knocked away). The firepit, pit kiln, log pile and charcoal pit, forge and anvil, quern, bloomery, blast furnace and barrel quests show the device with its numbered parts. The Lens Crown and the Great Works show stills of their Ponder scenes: click one to watch it.
- **Great Works,** a new optional group: The Gatehouse (Bronze Age: towers, a portcullis on a rope pulley, a drawbridge on a bearing, one lever), The Living House (Iron Age: sliding doors, a lift, lamps on a link, a board that speaks, a clock in the gable) and The Silo (Space Age: a 15 by 15 shaft, a sliding hatch and a rocket that rises from the ground). Each has a picture of the finished build, a labelled plan and item rewards; each appears with its Age.
- **Every Age builds the next.** The machines of a new Age take a part of the Age before, one part per step:
  - **Iron Age:** a hot ingot under the mechanical press gives two rods, as from the anvil. Three rubber bars make four belts.
  - **Arcane Age:** the Foliot and Djinni binding books take a precision mechanism in place of one dye (the mixer no longer binds them).
  - **Industrial Age:** insulated copper wire and conveyor belts take a rubber bar (latex from hevea or rubber fig, boiled with sulfur).
  - **Electric Age:** each generator block of the diesel generator holds an alternator. The huge diesel engine takes an Attuned Circuit in place of flint and steel. A new excavator vein holds native osmium with garnierite (new chunks only).
  - **Information Age:** the steel casing is built around a heavy engineering block; the anvil weld of osmium is gone. A basic control circuit is a circuit board with osmium and redstone (two per craft); four advanced circuits take four basic circuits, four infused alloys and one Attuned Circuit. The infuser makes neither any more. The hydrotreater cracks naphtha into ethene (1,000 mB from 1,500 mB) instead of petroleum gas. The ME controller holds a steel casing, the ME drive an advanced control circuit.
  - **Space Age:** the Digital Miner is built on the mechanical crafter. Tier 1 and 2 rockets burn diesel or kerosene, tier 3 and 4 rockets liquid hydrogen or cryo fuel. The elite tier installer takes a desh plate (it now belongs to the Space Age). The fission reactor port takes an advanced electrolysis core, the turbine casing steel sheet metal. The wyvern crafting injector takes an engineering processor, the wyvern core an advanced control circuit.
  - **Quantum Age:** the fusion reactor frames are built on the mechanical crafter. The ultimate tier installer takes a calorite plate (it now belongs to the Quantum Age). The fusion reactor controller holds a fission reactor port, the awakened core a 256k storage component in place of one wyvern core.
- **The Ore Processor** (Singularity Age), the last step of the ore ladder: six times the metal of an ore piece, as dust. Each dust takes 10 mB of hydrofluoric acid, 5 mB of kerosene and 500,000 FE (50,000 FE/t); a little lubricant doubles its speed for the same power. One block, fed by pipes, hoppers, chemical pipes or your hand; Jade shows what it waits for, the recipe viewer its recipes.
- **The Arcane Catalyst** (Information Age), magic as a bonus: set it against a Mekanism processing machine or factory or an Immersive Engineering electric multiblock, with a Source jar within six blocks and a turning shaft (64 RPM or more) beside it. The machine works 50 % faster and pays the power of each extra tick once more. Generators and miners do not take it.
- **Stargates need reactor power.** Dialling The Origin costs one billion FE and 50,000 FE/t while the wormhole is open; a gate within your own system costs 10 million FE and 5,000 FE/t. The Origin's return gate keeps one trip home charged, so nobody is stranded; its dialling device no longer holds a free fusion core (new arenas only).
- **One iron in loot and trade.** Chests and mob drops of every mod give wrought iron where they gave the vanilla iron ingot, and villagers and wandering traders trade wrought iron instead.
- **Antimatter costs a tenth of the polonium.** One pellet takes 100,000 mB of polonium and 40 billion FE (was 1,000,000 mB and 400 billion), about 50 minutes of a fusion plant with a turbine.
- **Steam engines power alternators again.** A Crafts & Additions alternator on a fuelled Create boiler gave no power: the check for fuelled rotation never found the steam engine. A level 18 boiler with 18 engines now gives its 3,024 FE/t.
- **Fission and fusion balanced** (measured on reference plants): a fission reactor burning 1 mB/t through a full turbine gives about 100,000 FE/t (was 80,000); fusion at injection 20 gives about 95,000 FE/t air-cooled and 840,000 FE/t through a turbine (was 4 million passive). Fusion now needs at least injection 20 water-cooled (10 air-cooled) to keep burning; the reactor's fuel tab shows the minimum.
- **The aurora is closer on warm worlds.** The cold band now starts at most about 1,000 blocks north of a warm spawn; worlds with a cold spawn keep it at home.
- **Wayshrine density per type** for new chunks (Forest Ring 72, Steppe Stones, Swamp Altar and Mountain Spire 80, Volcanic Ring 48 chunks apart): fewer worlds with very few or very many Wayshrines near spawn.
- **Macaw's glass and barn trapdoors** in vanilla wood come with the Iron Age, like their TerraFirmaCraft wood versions.
- **Fixed:** one steel ingot no longer presses into a steel sheet (a sheet is two ingots). Falling leaves of TerraFirmaCraft Regrowing Forests no longer load far chunks, which could stall a server for minutes while chunks were pregenerated.

## 0.12.0

The sky, the wood and the grid. The sky features are complete: the Lens Crown forges one celestial alloy per Age from the Arcane Age on, and each alloy goes into that Age's offering. Vanilla trees grow in the world again, and Dawn crafts on the ground. **A new world is best:** vanilla groves and the denser Wayshrines only appear in new chunks. An existing world keeps working; it gets one cluster of Drifting Isles near the shrine from the Industrial Age, and The Origin gains its plinths without touching your builds.

- **Crafting by hand in Dawn.** In Dawn your inventory has no crafting grid. Lay up to four items on the ground (place them with V on a block), then sneak and right-click the pile with an empty hand: it crafts like the grid would. The empty grid area says so, with your keys, and the first inventory you open in Dawn says it once in the action bar. The grid opens with The First Spark (Stone Age). New first quest: "By Hand".
- **Vanilla trees and wood classes.** Oak, birch, spruce, jungle, acacia, dark oak, cherry and mangrove grow again in TerraFirmaCraft forests, each in its own climate, and their saplings grow: always the plain tree, without bee nests, moss carpets or podzol around a giant spruce. Every naturally grown TerraFirmaCraft or ArborFirmaCraft trunk drops the vanilla log of its class (a placed log drops itself). Vanilla wood does what the species' wood does: a saw makes planks and the lead species' lumber, it burns and tans, it builds the lead species' supports, axles, water wheels and other TerraFirmaCraft function blocks, and four vanilla planks make a crafting table that works as a workbench from the Stone Age. Eight class logs or planks around a species sapling make eight of that species, so every look stays in reach. Vanilla wood decoration is back wherever a TerraFirmaCraft wood copy exists, in the same Age. New quests: "Wood Classes", "A Home" and "Mechanical Saw".
- **Sky Harvest:**
  - **Storm Rod** (Iron Age): a two-block rod with a meteoric iron tip. Fill it with 16 sand; in any rain, lightning strikes it now and then (four times as often in a thunderstorm) and fuses four sand into storm glass. No power needed, none made. Storm glass is clear, connected and blast resistant.
  - **Aurora Net** (Industrial Age): set it at Y 140 or higher under open sky in the cold north. Every clear night there has an aurora: the net glows and catches aurora light, a fluid you pipe or bucket out of it, twice as fast in the cold half of the year.
- **The Drifting Isles** (Industrial Age): islands of skystone float above the clouds, at Y 228 to 272. Reach them by airship or jetpack. They hold the only firmament crystal, in clusters and in one lode per cluster that stands again after you break it. One isle of each cluster carries the Wind Shrine, a Wayshrine of the sky. Skystone lets sky light through and spawns nothing.
- **The Launch Complex** (Space Age): build the gantry and its flame trench around a probe cradle, load a probe and a module into the launch control, fill it with rocket fuel or diesel and launch. The probe climbs, flies on its fuel and comes back with voidglass (sampler module) or deep-space samples (deep-space module, Quantum Age), the only sources of both. Pipes and ME networks can feed it through launch ports; redstone sets the throttle or holds the launch.
- **The Lens Crown is live** (from the Arcane Age): the Star Anvil under a chord of lenses forges the seven celestial alloys from sky materials and your Age's metal. Each goes into its Age's offering: Starlit Iron into the Arcane Keystone, Cloudsteel into the Pressure Core, Aurora Steel into the Humming Core, Halo Steel into the Data Matrix, Void Steel into the Star Chart, Quasar Alloy into the Quantum Core and the Firmament Alloy into the Ultimate Singularity. The Wayshrines take the alloys as their offerings from the Arcane to the Information Age.
- **The sky in the Chronicle.** From the Iron Age every Age has a Sky strand that names where each sky material comes from and ends in a keystone with its supplies: a falling star, the Storm Rod and storm glass; the Lens Crown and Starlit Iron; the Drifting Isles, the Aurora Net and Cloudsteel; starsteel, aurora light and Aurora Steel; Halo Steel; the Launch Complex, voidglass, Void Steel and the Sky Anchor; deep-space samples and Quasar Alloy; the Firmament Alloy. The recipe viewer has an information page for storm glass, firmament crystal, aurora light, voidglass, deep-space samples, both meteorite chunks and the meteorites, from their Age.
- **Two roads to the Arcane Keystone.** The Arcane goal opens with either route: the Spirit Route through the three magic strands, or the Machine Route with a Hollow Keystone filled on a machine line. Magic stays optional in the Chronicle too.
- **Energy upgrades in the Chronicle.** The Information Age teaches energy upgrades: without them the four and five times ore lines cost more power per ingot than purification.
- **Sky Anchors** (Space Age): a Sky Anchor carries the shrine's blessings within 24 blocks, in any dimension, on the Moon and Mars too. Set blessing shards into it; a shard works once its ring at the shrine is awakened.
- **The Origin Archipelago.** After The Primordial falls, each of the nine islands of The Origin has a plinth. Bring it samples of its Age (plants, a led animal, a running machine of that Age on the island, and from the Arcane island on a crown of four alloy ingots) and the island grows in three stages; a finished island lights a beam at the altar. The islands grow from the pack's own blocks (a wrought iron anvil and lamp, a firepit, TerraFirmaCraft flowers and soil). Nothing in the main game depends on it.
- **Proof of work at the shrine.** From the Industrial Age on, an offering first needs a tithe of the Age's metal fed into Tally Chutes in that Age: 384 steel ingots, then 512 aluminium, 1,024 osmium, 2,880 desh and 3,584 calorite, about two hours of one production line of that Age. The Tally Chute (now an Industrial Age block) takes only what Caelum still asks, from any side, a pipe or your hand, and keeps it, so nothing counts twice; a stockpile counts when you feed it. Caelum names what is missing; each Age's first quest names the proof, a "Proof of Work" quest and Jade on the chute or the heart show the count. The heart also asks for power: 10 million FE at the Data Nave, 100 million at the Quantum Ring and one billion for the Gathering.
- **The Primordial by numbers.** One hit deals at most 40 damage, so a full damage-module sword no longer ends the fight in a minute. The fight lasts about eight minutes alone, seven for two and six and a half for four; the phases begin at 69 % and 38 % of its health.
- **More Wayshrines.** They lie a little closer together (spacing 72 instead of 80 chunks): 5 to 16 within 4,000 blocks of spawn. A ruin holds only blocks you could find in Dawn: the iron bars of the Swamp Altar, the Mountain Spire and the Wind Shrine were stolen, and the Reef Altar's corals turned to limestone.
- **One ore ladder.** Every way of turning ore into metal now sits on one step of a ladder, and every Age climbs it: crushing wheels give 1.5 times the metal of the crucible (was 1.7), spagyrics 2 times, the Immersive Engineering crusher 2.5 times (was 2) plus mineral powder and a small chance of a second metal, Purification 3 times, Injection 4 times and Dissolution 5 times. The Mekanism steps take TerraFirmaCraft ore pieces of every grade.
- **Mineral powder is not waste.** From the Information Age the enrichment chamber turns 64 mineral powder from the crusher or the quern into a metal dust. The arc furnace no longer melts powder.
- **The arc furnace is the Electric Age smelter.** It melts any metal dust into its ingot for 6,400 FE (was 51,200).
- **One iron.** Wrought iron is the pack's iron. The vanilla iron ingot is gone: every recipe that asked for it takes wrought iron. Iron dust smelts into cast iron in a furnace or on a fan, and into wrought iron only in the arc furnace and the energized smelter.
- **The Dimensional Mineshaft** brings up ore pieces of every grade (mostly poor and normal) instead of only rich ones.
- **Fuel has one value.** A bucket of fuel gives the same energy in every generator that burns it: creosote 82 thousand FE, kerosene 0.85 million, diesel 1.32 million, ethylene 11.3 million. Every diesel engine burns at the rate that gives this value through an alternator: the modular engine burns faster (it gave 1.68 million FE per bucket), the turbocharged engine burns twice as fast as it turns.
- **Mekanism machines need real power.** Every Mekanism and Mekanism: More Machine machine uses ten times the energy (200 to 1,600 FE/t); energy upgrades matter.
- **Miners are not free ore.** The Digital Miner uses 4,800 FE/t and takes 10 seconds per block without upgrades (energy and speed upgrades work as before; fully upgraded it mines a block a second for 48,000 FE/t, well inside one fission reactor with a turbine), about 1.5 times the excavator's energy per ore. The excavator digs at half speed for the same power. Every step of the ore ladder lowers the energy per ingot, as long as your Mekanism machines carry energy upgrades.

## 0.11.0

The content of 1.0 is locked: no new mods after this round. The final boss of The Origin is back as the pack's own, stars fall from the sky, ruined shrines wait in the wide world, and power and ore follow one ladder. **Start a new world:** Wayshrines and old craters only generate in new chunks, and two mods left.

- **The Primordial,** the guardian of The Origin. Gather at the altar, all of you, and it wakes: a giant of dark glass with a veil that swallows most of your blows. The veil opens only when you answer its attacks (strike the keel after a slam, shoot the glowing node, catch the shards of the Firmament Shatter). Three phases, six to ten minutes for a small fellowship. Its fall grants the finale as before. New in the Chronicle: "The Primordial" (how to open the veil) and the side quest "A Piece of the Sky" (four glass and a shard make four Firmament Glass). Keep at least one Consecrated Shard: they fall only in its last phase, lie a minute and never come again once it is beaten. The shard's tooltip names its three uses.
- **Starfall.** From the Iron Age a star falls on some clear nights: a streak toward its real bearing, a boom, and a smoking crater 300 to 2,500 blocks away. Its meteorites are the only meteoric iron (Iron Age; heat it and work it on the anvil into ingots, double ingots and sheets) and, from the Electric Age, starsteel (arc furnace); both are the Wayshrine offerings of their Ages. While the core glows, two days, every meteorite of that crater drops double. Old cold craters lie in new chunks, and from the Information Age an excavator finds a meteoric vein at a crater. Stars never fall near the shrine, a respawn point or claimed land.
- **Wayshrines.** Ruined shrines of Caelum lie in the wide world, eight kinds, each in its own climate and built from that place's stone; in a new world the first lies a few hundred blocks from spawn. Right-click the shrine's heart to learn the bearing of the nearest one. Rebuild it from the ghost, lay the offering of your Age, do its rite and pray: a lit Wayshrine carries the shrine's blessings within 24 blocks and drops a Wayshrine Stone the first time. The shrine's blessings no longer reach everywhere; the Wayshrines carry them. Every Age can light them: a bronze double ingot in the Bronze Age, then sky metal from Starfall (meteoric iron ingot in the Iron Age, its double ingot in the Arcane Age, its sheet in the Industrial Age, a starsteel ingot from the Electric Age on). The celestial alloys of the Lens Crown take over these offerings when it arrives in a later round.
- **New mods by Age:**
  - **Dawn and Stone Age:** Alex's Mobs Continued (22 animals TerraFirmaCraft lacks, each in its own climate), Goblin Traders (in caves, trading TerraFirmaCraft goods of your Age, no treasure maps).
  - **More for the home:** Armor Statues and Banner Flags (Stone Age; sneak and right-click a fence or wall with a banner), Connected Glass, Advanced Chimneys and To the Bat Poles! (Bronze Age; the redstone lift pole in the Iron Age), Bibliocraft Legacy (Iron Age: display cases in the oak look from any wooden slab, labels, clipboard, stockroom catalog, bookcases and fancy signs in every TerraFirmaCraft and ArborFirmaCraft wood, typewriters and the printing table; its seats, tables, shelves, lamps, clocks, fancy armor stands, tool rack and sword pedestal are not in the pack, and the printing table no longer copies enchanted books), KleeSlabs (break one half of a double slab), Akashic Tome (Arcane Age: every guide book in one tome) and Enchantment Descriptions.
  - **A handful of acorns:** long secateurs (Bronze Age, brass mechanism) cut acorns, chestnuts, hickory nuts and coconuts from TerraFirmaCraft trees in autumn; roast them in a firepit.
  - **Chimneys for TerraFirmaCraft:** the smoke of a lit firepit, grill, pot, bloomery, blast furnace or charcoal forge rises through a chimney built above it. Smoke never poisons anyone.
  - **On the client:** 3D Skin Layers, Auroras (northern lights towards the cold north; the Complementary shader's own aurora is off in new installs), Cool Rain, BetterF3 (it hides the coordinates in the Stone Age like the plain debug screen), Chat Heads and Big Sign Writer.
  - **Artifacts** (from the Bronze Age): trinkets in the chests of TerraFirmaCraft ruins, the Cold Forge and the Nether fortresses, more of them in later Ages. Eight artifacts are not in the pack.
  - **Arcane Age:** Ars Elemental, Ars Ocultas and Ars Elemancy (from the Electric Age); their armour upgrades take materials, not a second set of armour.
  - **Iron Age:** MmmMmmMmmMmm (target dummies that show your damage).
  - **Industrial Age:** Create: Extra Gauges.
  - **Electric Age:** AeroEngine (jet engines for your airships, aviation kerosene from Immersive Petroleum kerosene; no missiles) and Building Gadgets 2 (building and exchanging gadget on FE; the exchanger refuses ores and raw rock; no copy and paste).
  - **Information Age:** Psi, the programming magic (osmium parts, a CAD assembler with two Mekanism control circuits) and Ars Énergistique (Source in your ME network).
  - **Singularity Age:** Draconic Additions, only its three necklaces.
- **Removed:** Carry On and MrCrayfish's Furniture: Refurbished.
- **Power has a price.** The alternator gives FE only for rotation from burning fuel: a steam engine on a boiler over a fuelled blaze burner, or a diesel engine. Water wheels, windmills and hand cranks turn it but give no FE; on a shaft with both, it gives the fuelled share. At full fuel it gives 168 FE/t at 256 RPM instead of 360. Lava no longer feeds a blaze burner, and the huge diesel engine burns diesel about twice as fast: every diesel engine now gives about as much FE per bucket as the diesel generator (1.3 to 1.7 million FE). Plant oil and ethanol no longer run diesel engines. The crystal resonance generator, the Applied Kinetics energy provider and the solar upgrade are gone; your ME network runs on the power of your Age. The thermoelectric boiler, the industrial turbine and the fuelwood and resistive heaters come with the Space Age.
- **No metal without ore.** Metal essence of Mystical Agriculture grows TerraFirmaCraft ore pieces (eight essence give two normal pieces) instead of ingots, so your ore line multiplies it like any other ore; netherite essence gives ancient debris. Hostile Neural Networks gives ore pieces instead of iron, copper and gold ingots. Reformation no longer makes osmium or uranium, and earth, gem and mineral niter no longer turn into metal. The Gambler spirit trades gems for gems, not for nuggets. Washing gravel, soul sand and red sand no longer gives iron or gold nuggets.
- **Spagyrics on every grade.** Ore pieces of every grade digest in the digestion vat into alchemical sulfur at exactly twice the metal (ten rich pieces give seven sulfur, two normal pieces one); the liquefaction cauldron no longer takes ore.
- **Draconium and planet ores join the ore line.** End draconium ore drops two rich draconium pieces instead of dust; iron, copper and gold ores of the Moon, Mars, Mercury, Venus and Glacio drop rich TerraFirmaCraft ore pieces instead of raw metal. Excavator veins (banded iron, lazulitic intrusion, rich auricupride, mephitic quarzite) give TerraFirmaCraft ore pieces; the alluvial sift gives rough gems, the super vein rough emeralds. All of them now form in TerraFirmaCraft lands: rich auricupride in its badlands, the alluvial sift and the super vein under rivers and lakes.
- **Applied Energistics 2:** every ME cable carries four times the channels (32, dense cable 128), and every item storage cell holds 126 item types instead of 63 (portable cells keep theirs). The wireless crafting terminal comes in the Industrial Age with an expensive recipe on the mechanical crafter: until it is linked, right-click opens a portable 3 by 3 crafting grid. It links to an ME network from the Information Age on, when the cheaper recipe opens. Autocrafting stays in the Information Age.
