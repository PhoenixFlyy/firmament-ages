# Changelog lines of h3/links (harmony package A9)

For the next version section of `CHANGELOG.md`, player-facing English.

- **Every Age builds the next.** The machines of a new Age now take a part of the Age before, one part per step:
  - **Iron Age:** a hot ingot under the mechanical press gives two rods, as from the anvil. Three rubber bars make four belts.
  - **Arcane Age:** the Foliot and Djinni binding books take a precision mechanism in place of one dye (the mixer no longer binds them).
  - **Industrial Age:** insulated copper wire and conveyor belts take a rubber bar (latex from hevea or rubber fig, boiled with sulfur).
  - **Electric Age:** each generator block of the diesel generator holds an alternator. The huge diesel engine takes an Attuned Circuit in place of flint and steel. A new excavator vein holds native osmium with garnierite (new chunks only).
  - **Information Age:** the steel casing is built around a heavy engineering block; the anvil weld of osmium is gone. A basic control circuit is a circuit board with osmium and redstone (two per craft); four advanced circuits take four basic circuits, four infused alloys and one Attuned Circuit. The infuser makes neither any more. The hydrotreater cracks naphtha into ethene (1,000 mB from 1,500 mB) instead of petroleum gas. The ME controller holds a steel casing, the ME drive an advanced control circuit.
  - **Space Age:** the Digital Miner is built on the mechanical crafter. Tier 1 and 2 rockets burn diesel or kerosene, tier 3 and 4 rockets liquid hydrogen or cryo fuel. The elite tier installer takes a desh plate (it now belongs to the Space Age). The fission reactor port takes an advanced electrolysis core, the turbine casing steel sheet metal. The wyvern crafting injector takes an engineering processor, the wyvern core an advanced control circuit.
  - **Quantum Age:** the fusion reactor frames are built on the mechanical crafter. The ultimate tier installer takes a calorite plate (it now belongs to the Quantum Age). The fusion reactor controller holds a fission reactor port, the awakened core a 256k storage component in place of one wyvern core.
- **Fix:** one steel ingot no longer presses into a steel sheet (a sheet is two ingots).
